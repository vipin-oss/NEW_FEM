## What this list does and does not say

Every entry is reproducible: `python3 python/run_all.py` regenerates it, and each is graded by a criterion that compares it with something else in the same solver (mesh refinement, a witness run, a $\mu=0$ floor).  That is a statement about consistency, not about correctness against nature.  The values in Table 6 are the ones where an outside route exists; these are the ones where it does not.

Two entries deserve a caveat larger than the rest, and both are in §7 of the manuscript: the flexoelectric numbers are proportional to an unmeasured $\mu^2$, and every frequency is proportional to an unsourced $\rho^{-1/2}$.
