The order of convergence is the least-squares slope of
$\log|u_R/u_{\rm ref}-1|$ against $\log h$, with $h=L/n_x$ halved over the
four meshes $5\times1$, $10\times2$, $20\times4$ and $40\times8$ of the
gradient-free benchmark; the slope over all
four is 1.68 and the finest pair alone gives 2.01, so the sequence is entering
its asymptotic range from the coarse end. The expected rate for the bicubic
Hermite rectangle is $O(h^{4})$ in $L^2$ and $O(h^{3})$ in $H^1$ on a smooth
solution, under suitable regularity assumptions. The observed tip-displacement rate is
a benchmark-specific result; corner regularity and derivative constraints
can affect convergence, so these data do not prove a universal rate.

Table~\ref{tab:convergence} gives the underlying mesh-refinement values. The frequency of the extensional mode and its
mode-free coupling factor are listed for four meshes, with the errors referred
to the $48\times4$ reference mesh: the coarser $24\times2$ mesh of the
parameter sweeps differs from it by $0.04\,\%$ in frequency and by
$0.03\,\%$ in the coupling factor, the $36\times2$ row differs by less, while the $12\times2$ row has larger
errors. All four calculations use the left-edge constraints of the assembly
routine. A separate clamped-strip comparison
compares the $96\times4$ and $48\times4$ meshes; their coupling factors
differ by $5.9\times10^{-4}$ relative.
